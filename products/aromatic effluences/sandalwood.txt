The Complete Export Play‑Book for Sandalwood Essential Oil (Santalum album) – “Made in India” (2024‑2025)
Why Sandalwood?
Sandalwood oil is the world’s most valuable “red gold” fragrance ingredient.  • Average FOB $1 200 – $2 000 / kg (pure, 70 % α‑santalol).
 • Demand from ultra‑luxury perfumery, high‑end cosmetics, aromatherapy, natural‑medicine and niche‑wellness markets.
 • India (Kashmir‑grown Santalum album) supplies ≈ 80 % of the world’s premium oil and enjoys the Geographical Indication (GI) “Kashmir Sandalwood”.

Below is a step‑by‑step guide that takes you from raw heart‑wood sourcing to delivering a certified, premium product to customers in the USA, EU, GCC, Japan, South‑Korea, etc.

3️⃣ SOURCING THE RAW HEART‑WOOD
Step	Action	Practical Tips
Identify licensed growers	• Kashmir Sandalwood Development Corporation (KSDC)
• J&K Horticulture Dept.
• Private cultivators listed with APEDA.
• Must hold a CITES permit (Sandalwood is a CITES‑regulated species).	Verify CITES registration numbers for each orchard.
Supply agreement	Define quantity (kg of heart‑wood), age of trees (≥ 15 years), moisture ≤ 12 %, price, delivery schedule, penalties for contamination, traceability (lot‑number → farmer).	Secure 2‑3 independent contracts to reduce climate/political risk.
Harvest & post‑harvest handling	• Fell trees carefully, cut heart‑wood blocks (≈ 30 cm × 15 cm × 15 cm).
• Air‑dry in shaded, ventilated sheds for 6‑12 months to reach ≤ 12 % moisture.
• Store in jute bags with silica‑gel packets, in a cool (≤ 22 °C), low‑RH warehouse.	Moisture control is critical – high moisture leads to microbial growth and oil‑yield loss.
At‑farm QC	Visual inspection, moisture test (digital hygrometer). Random HPLC sample for α‑santalol (target ≥ 65 % in raw wood).	Keep a Batch‑Log Sheet linking each lot number to farmer, date, moisture, and preliminary test results.
Typical lead‑time: 7‑10 days from contract signing to raw heart‑wood arrival at your processing unit.

4️⃣ PROCESSING & VALUE‑ADDITION
Process	Options	Recommended Set‑up (sourcing model)
Steam distillation	• Traditional copper pot still (30 °C‑40 °C).
• Modern stainless‑steel column still with fractionating trays (better α‑santalol recovery).	Contract a GMP‑certified distillation plant (e.g., Sabinsa, Aroha Biotech, Mediherb) that can guarantee ≥ 70 % α‑santalol for premium grades.
Fractionation	Low‑temperature vacuum distillation or molecular‑distillation to concentrate α‑santalol (70 %‑90 %).	Required for Category I (≥ 70 % α‑santalol) oils used by top‑tier perfumers.
Refinement	• Degumming (removal of waxes, gums).
• Winterisation (cold‑filtering) → clearer, more stable oil.	Use stainless‑steel filter press; run micro‑filtration (0.2 µm) for cosmetic‑grade purity.
Standardisation	Adjust blend to target α‑santalol % (70 % or 90 %) by mixing fractions.	Perform GC‑MS on each batch; keep variance < 3 % (SPC).
Final product formats	1. Pure essential oil – 10 ml, 30 ml, 100 ml amber glass ampoules (most common).
2. Bulk drums (20 L) for B2B perfume houses.
3. Niche blends (e.g., sandalwood‑vetiver‑citrus) for private‑label.	Start with 10 ml and 30 ml ampoules for luxury retail; add bulk drums later for B2B.
Packaging	• Amber‑glass ampoules with nitrogen flush (removes residual oxygen).
• UV‑protective screw caps with tamper‑evident seal.
• Secondary box – FSC‑certified cardboard, QR‑code linking to CoA and farmer story.	Choose recyclable & premium packaging – a strong marketing lever for “clean‑beauty”.
Typical processing time: 2‑4 days from raw wood receipt to sealed ampoule (including QC).

Typical timelines:

Organic – 60‑90 days.
Fair‑Trade – 30‑45 days.
CITES permit – 30‑60 days after source verification.

8️⃣ LOGISTICS & FREIGHT
Mode	When to Use	Key Points
Air freight (express)	High‑value, low‑volume orders (< 5 kg) – e.g., boutique perfume houses, DTC kits.	Use temperature‑controlled containers (≤ 25 °C). Pack ampoules in foam‑lined sealed cartons.
Sea freight – LCL	Bulk shipments (10‑30 tons) for large perfume manufacturers.	Dry‑air‑conditioned containers (temperature ≤ 25 °C) to avoid oxidation.
Sea freight – FCL	Very large contracts (> 30 tons) – e.g., multinational cosmetics conglomerates.	Same dry‑air‑conditioned container; use double‑wall pallets for extra protection.
Insurance	All shipments.	Insure for CIF value + 10 %; for ultra‑premium sandalwood, choose all‑risk cargo insurance.
Customs clearance	Destination country.	Use an experienced customs broker (e.g., Kuehne + Nagel, Expeditors) familiar with HS code 3301 90 (essential oils).
Typical lead‑times:

India → USA (air): 5‑7 days door‑to‑door.
India → EU (sea): 35‑50 days + 5‑7 days customs.
Air: 5‑7 days – fastest for DTC orders.

10.3 Sales Funnel (B2B)
Initial Inquiry – Send sample kit (1 ml sealed vial) + digital CoA (α‑santalol %, heavy‑metal).
Technical Evaluation – Answer R&D questions: oil profile (GC‑MS), IFRA usage limits, CITES documentation, shelf‑life data.
Negotiation – Discuss MOQ (5 kg – 20 kg for first order), price (e.g., $1 600 / kg FOB organic, 70 % α‑santalol), payment terms (30 % T/T upfront, 70 % against B/L).
Contract – Draft a Purchase Agreement covering product specs, QC acceptance (≤ 3 % variance), warranty, force‑majeure, arbitration (ICC).
Production & QC – Run pilot batch, issue batch CoA & SDS; share with buyer for pre‑shipment approval.
Shipping – Forward BL, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, CO, CITES Permit, SDS to buyer’s freight forwarder.
Post‑sale – Provide technical support (e.g., blending tips), request feedback, collect testimonials. Offer re‑order discount (5‑10 % for repeat within 6 months).
