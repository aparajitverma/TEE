Assam Black Tea (CTC & Orthodox Flush‑1/2) 

Target audience: Tea growers, estate managers, contract factories, and export‑households that are already processing or intend to process Assam CTC/Orthodox tea for the Flush‑1/2 segment.

1. Quick Business Snapshot
Item	Detail
Product code	CTC – “Crush‑Tea‑Crush” pellets; Orthodox – whole‑leaf/broken‑leaf fine.
Flush	Flush‑1 (March – May) – highest catechin & aromatic value; Flush‑2 (June – July) – slightly lower price but still premium.
Typical yield	2 000 – 2 500 kg green‑leaf ha⁻¹ (fresh weight).
Export price (FOB 2023‑24)	US $2.80‑$3.20 kg⁻¹ for Flush‑1 CTC, US $3.40‑$4.00 kg⁻¹ for Premium Orthodox.
Key markets	USA, UK, Germany, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Japan, Kenya (re‑export).
HS‑Code	0901 10 00 (Assam black tea, CTC/Orthodox).
Regulatory anchors	IEC (DGFT), APEDA Export Licence, NPOP (Food‑Safety Standards), ISO 22000/ISO 9001, optional Fair‑Trade, Rainforest Alliance, Organic (APEDA‑Organic), GI‑tag (Only for Darjeeling – not applicable to Assam but useful for branding.
2. Legal & Certification Checklist
Requirement	Why It Matters	How to Obtain / Maintain
IEC (Import‑Export Code)	Mandatory for any foreign trade.	Apply on DGFT – IEC portal (2‑3 days).
APEDA Export Licence	Grants access to export incentives, organic/halal certifications, buyer‑trust.	Submit PAN, GST, IEC, bank details; receive licence online.
NPOP – Food‑Safety Standard for Tea	Sets permissible limits for pesticide residues, heavy metals, aflatoxin.	Laboratory testing (NABL‑accredited) and compliance report.
ISO 22000 / ISO 9001	International food‑safety & quality‑management.	Implement QMS, schedule internal/audit, obtain certification from a recognised registrar.
Optional Certifications	Fair‑Trade, Rainforest Alliance, Organic (APEDA‑Organic) – command premium.	Apply via certifying bodies; annual audits and traceability records.
Phytosanitary Certificate	Required by most importing customs for plant‑origin goods.	Apply through APEDA – Plant Quarantine after field inspection.

3. Agronomy – From Seed to Leaf
Stage	Technical Parameters	Best‑Practice Controls
Planting material	Certified Camellia sinensis var. assamica seedlings (12‑14 months old).	Source from ICAR‑NBRI or APEDA‑approved nurseries; maintain a seed‑lot register.
Soil & pH	Sandy‑loam, well‑drained, pH 5.5‑6.5.	Conduct quarter‑yearly soil tests; amend with organic compost (3‑5 t ha⁻¹) + N‑PK (80‑40‑60 kg ha⁻¹).
Irrigation	Drip, 0.5‑0.7 L m⁻² day⁻¹ after establishment; reduce during monsoon.	Install soil‑moisture sensors for precise dosing.
Fertilisation	Basal: 80 kg N ha⁻¹, 40 kg P₂O₅ ha⁻¹, 60 kg K₂O ha⁻¹; top‑dress N at 6‑month intervals.	Follow ICAR‑recommended schedule; keep a fertiliser application log.
Pest & Disease Management	Main threats: mite (Eriophyidae), blister blight (Exobasidium vexans), root rot (Phytophthora spp.).	Adopt IPM – Trichoderma‑based bio‑fungicides, neem‑based miticides; keep a pesticide‑use register complying with NPOP limits (< 0.01 mg kg⁻¹ for most residues).
Plucking regime	Two‑leaf‑and‑a‑bud for high‑quality CTC; three‑leaf‑bud for Orthodox.	Train pluckers, use hand‑held data‑capture devices to record pluck time, plume, and plot ID.
Harvest timing (Flush‑1)	Early March – mid‑May; leaf bud‑burst phase, highest polyphenol content.	Use phenological calendar and leaf‑color index (NDVI / satellite imagery) to schedule.
Traceability	Assign Batch‑Lot ID: ASS‑CTC‑FL1‑YYMM‑NN (or ASS‑ORT‑FL2‑YYMM‑NN). QR‑code on each crate, GPS‑tagged field.	Integrate with ERP / LIMS for downstream visibility.
4. Processing – CTC vs. Orthodox (Flush‑1/2)
4.1 Common Pre‑Distillation Steps
Step	Parameter	Comments
Withering	25‑28 °C, 65‑70 % RH, 12‑14 h.	Reduces moisture to 65‑70 % fresh weight; facilitates pliability.
Leaf Sorting	Mechanical graded‑sorting → remove twigs, foreign matter.	Use optical sorters (infrared) for large estates.
Moisture check	Target 60‑65 % (post‑withering).	Verify with Moisture Analyzer (ASTM D 4442).
4.2 CTC (Crush‑Tea‑Crush) Flow
Operation	Technical Detail	Typical Yield (kg oil kg⁻¹)
Rolling (CTC roller)	Two‑roller cylindrical press, pressure ≈ 80‑100 MPa, roll‑speed ≈ 150 rpm.	80‑85 % leaf‑mass retained after rolling.
Crushing & Cutting	Counter‑rotating blades cut the rolled leaf into 3‑5 mm diameter pellets (CTC “fannings”).	Produces uniform CTC granules → rapid infusion.
Fermentation (oxidation)	25‑28 °C, 95‑100 % RH, 30‑45 min (for Flush‑1).	Fully oxidised; catechins → theaflavins (TF) & thearubigins (TR).
Drying (kiln/air‑dryer)	80‑90 °C, 4‑5 h (to ≤ 3 % moisture).	Fixes oxidation level; reduces microbial risk.
Sorting & Grading	BOP (Broken Orange Pekoe), OP (Orange Pekoe), PF (Pekoe Fannings), GF (Golden Fannings).	Grader‑controlled by color, size, impurity %.
Bulk Packing	Jute bags (50 kg) or PP woven bags (45 kg), vented for moisture control.	Label with Batch‑Lot ID, moisture % and grade.
4.3 Orthodox (Whole‑Leaf/Broken‑Leaf) Flow
Operation	Technical Detail	Typical Yield
Rolling (Orthodox roller)	Light‑pressure roll (≈ 30‑40 MPa), roll‑speed ≈ 80 rpm; leaves are twisted not crushed.	Preserves leaf integrity.
Fermentation (oxidation)	25‑28 °C, 95‑100 % RH, 45‑60 min (Flush‑1).	Higher TF:TR ratio → brighter liquor.
Drying	80‑90 °C, 5‑6 h.	Moisture ≤ 3 %.
Sorting	Whole‑leaf grades – FTGFOP (Fine TGFOP), SFTGFOP (Super FTGFOP); Broken‑leaf – BP (Broken Pekoe), BOP1/2.	Visual + optical sorter (size‑filter).
Packing	PP‑lined jute bags (50 kg) for whole‑leaf; bulk PP bags (45 kg) for broken.	Same labeling as CTC.
Note: For Export‑Grade Flush‑1, the oxidation index (measured by spectrophotometric Theaflavin + Thearubigin absorbance, 380 nm) should be ≥ 1400 (per NPOP).

5. Quality Assurance (QA) – Laboratory & In‑Process Controls
Parameter	Method (ISO/ASTM)	Acceptable Limits (Flush‑1)
Moisture (wet basis)	ASTM D 4442 (Karl‑Fischer)	3.0 % ± 0.5 %
Ash content	ISO 6571	≤ 2.5 %
Caffeine	HPLC (ISO 17025)	2.5‑3.5 % w/w
Theaflavin (TF) + Thearubigin (TR) index	Spectrophotometric (abs 380 nm)	≥ 1400 (Flush‑1)
Total polyphenols	Folin‑Ciocalteu (ISO 17025)	70‑80 % of dry weight
Pesticide residues	LC‑MS/MS (NPOP)	Below MRL for each pesticide (e.g., chlorpyrifos ≤ 0.01 mg kg⁻¹)
Heavy metals (Pb, Cd, As, Hg)	ICP‑MS (ISO 17294‑2)	Pb ≤ 0.1 ppm, Cd ≤ 0.05 ppm, As ≤ 0.2 ppm, Hg ≤ 0.01 ppm
Microbial load	ISO 20776‑1 (total plate count)	≤ 10³ CFU g⁻¹
Sensory grading	Certified tea‑taster panel (ISO 13312) – colour, aroma, liquor, briskness.	Must meet buyer‑specified sensory profile.
CoA (Certificate of Analysis) is prepared for each container, signed by the Head of QA and uploaded to the Export Dossier.
Batch traceability: LIMS links every QA result to the Batch‑Lot ID printed on the bag.
6. Packaging, Preservation & Labelling
Item	Specification	Reason
Primary pack	Jute bag (50 kg), 100 % natural fibre, ventilated holes (5 mm) – or PP woven bag (45 kg) for higher moisture barrier.	Allows breathability, prevents moisture build‑up, complies with BIS IS 3044.
Secondary pack	Four‑wall corrugated cartons (1800 × 1200 × 1000 mm) with inner foam inserts.	Shock protection for sea/road handling.
Label content (mandatory)	• Product name (e.g., “Assam Flush‑1 CTC – BOP”)
• Batch‑Lot ID
• Net weight
• Moisture %
• Grade
• Manufacturer & address
• “Made in India”
• FSSAI licence No.
• Optional certifications (Fair‑Trade, Organic)
• QR‑code linking to traceability page.	Meets BIS IS 3024, EU Regulation 1223/2009, US CFR 21.
Barcoding / RFID	GS1‑128 linear barcode on each carton + optional UHF RFID tag.	Enables customs scanning & buyer’s WMS integration.
Preservation	Store at 15‑20 °C, < 70 % RH, away from direct sunlight. Use desiccant sachets (silica gel, 0.5 % w/w) for long shipments (> 30 days).	Prevents oxidation & moisture ingress; maintains aroma.
7. Export Documentation (End‑to‑End Flow)
Commercial Invoice – FOB/CIF, HS‑code, value, payment terms, bank details.
Packing List – detailed description, bag numbers, gross/net weight, dimensions.
Certificate of Origin (CO) – issued by Chamber of Commerce, includes IEC.
Phytosanitary Certificate – from APEDA Plant Quarantine (valid 30 days).
Certificate of Analysis (CoA) – batch‑specific QC results (see Section 5).
Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) – for bulk shipments (optional, but requested by some EU buyers).
Letter of Credit (LC) / Documentary Credit – attach CoA & Phytosanitary.
Insurance Certificate – All‑Risks (CIF) covering loss, spoilage.
Export Declaration (Shipping Bill) – filed on ICEGATE, generates Bill of Export (BE) number.
FSSAI Import Permit (if buyer asks for nutraceutical / functional‑food use).
All documents should be digitally archived (PDF, signed electronic) and shared via secure portal (e.g., DocuWare, SharePoint) with the buyer and freight forwarder.

8. Logistics & Freight Management
Decision point	Options & Recommendations
Incoterm	FOB (Ramgarh/Port of Kolkata) is standard for tea; CIF if buyer wants freight & insurance handled.
Freight mode	Containerised sea freight – 20‑ft or 40‑ft HC (≈ 1 200 kg per 20‑ft, 2 000 kg per 40‑ft).
Air freight only for urgent “sample” batches (high‑value Orthodox).
Container loading	Load bags on pallets (1200 × 1000 mm), max height 1.2 m.
Secure with strap‑bands; apply seal number on shipping documents.
Temperature & humidity	Not a temperature‑sensitive cargo, but avoid > 30 °C and > 80 % RH to prevent mold.
For long voyages (> 30 days) add silica‑gel packets.
Tracking	Install GPS tracker plus optional humidity datalogger (Smart‑Tag). Share live location with buyer via portal.
Customs clearance (India)	Random inspections possible; keep CoA, MSDS, Phytosanitary ready. Pay IGST (if applicable) and claim refund under GST Export Scheme.
Customs clearance (Destination)	Provide Bill of Lading, Invoice, CO, CoA, LC. Most top markets apply zero duty for HS 0901.00.
Insurance	“All‑Risks” covering Total Loss, Partial Loss, Moisture Damage – advisable for high‑value Orthodox.
9. Marketing & Market‑Entry Tips
Action	Practical Steps
Brand positioning	Emphasise “Assam Flush‑1 – Indian ‘Morning Brew’”; use storytelling of “wild monsoon forests”.
Buyer segmentation	• CTC – bulk‑blend manufacturers (tea‑bag producers)
• Orthodox – specialty tea houses, premium hotels, organic chains.
Sample strategy	Send 3 kg of each grade (BOP, OP, PF) in sterile, sealed cartons with a digital CoA. Follow up within 48 h.
Pricing	Base FOB on TCI (Tea Cost Index) plus processing margin; add premium for organic or fair‑trade certification.
Trade shows	Attend World Tea Expo (USA), ITMA (Berlin), Tea & Coffee Asia (Hong Kong) – use them to secure forward contracts.
Digital presence	Maintain B2B portal (Alibaba, TradeIndia) with QR‑code‑linked traceability; update with batch photos and test results.
Risk mitigation	• Weather‑hedge via crop‑insurance (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana).
• Price‑hedge using commodity futures on MCX (if available) or OTC contracts with banks.
